# N02 | Draft IMA — Schedule A, investment guidelines and service annex

SYNTHETIC DEMO MATERIAL — not a Capital Group record, policy, client or commitment.

Case: NEW | Date: 2026-09-10 | Owner: Legal | Version: 3.0 | Status: current

## N02§1 — Tobacco exclusion

The Manager shall not purchase securities issued by companies deriving more than 5% of annual revenue from tobacco production. The schedule is silent on parent aggregation, data provider and unavailable revenue data.

## N02§2 — Thermal coal exclusion

Investment in companies involved in thermal coal is prohibited. This draft does not define extraction, power generation, distribution, a revenue threshold, or treatment of mixed business models.

## N02§3 — Reporting and derivatives

Weekly CHF performance, attribution and ESG analytics shall be delivered by Monday 08:00 Zurich. FX forwards and interest-rate futures are within the proposed permitted instruments. This document is unsigned; client correspondence alone does not update the contract.

## N02§4 — Draft precedence

This is IMA draft version 3, not an executed instruction. Resolve conflicts against the client policy with Legal and the client. Prior client contract language is a precedent for review, not authority to modify this mandate.
