## O03§1 — Field semantics

TOB_PROD_REV_PCT: annual tobacco-production revenue percentage at the selected issuer mapping level. COAL_EXTRACT_REV_PCT: thermal-coal extraction revenue percentage. COAL_POWER_REV_PCT is a different field; extraction cannot stand in for generation. Missing provider values must remain null.

## O03§2 — Interpretation required

Legal and FI PC must confirm activity definition, threshold operator, denominator, issuer/parent scope, provider, as-of date, missing-data behavior, passive breaches and any exemptions. Prior implementation code is evidence of one client interpretation, not a current instruction.
