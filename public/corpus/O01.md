# O01 | Reporting service capability matrix

SYNTHETIC DEMO MATERIAL — not a Capital Group record, policy, client or commitment.

Case: OPS | Date: 2026-09-08 | Owner: Client Reporting | Version: 2.0 | Status: current

## O01§1 — Available service

For the synthetic global IG bond platform, CHF holdings can be produced weekly on Tuesday by 12:00, subject to a validated source feed. Validated performance, attribution and ESG analytics are available monthly on the fifth business day. Weekly analytics by Monday 08:00 are not a supported standard service.

## O01§2 — Dependency and approval

Attribution needs validated transactions, benchmark returns, currency calculations and reconciliation. Holdings availability alone does not prove attribution feasibility. A bespoke weekly service requires a separate feasibility assessment and authorized commercial/service acceptance; no committed build date exists.

## O01§3 — Version authority

Version 2 supersedes O02 for new onboarding plans. Historical examples remain useful for failure patterns and preventive actions, but cannot establish current platform capability.
