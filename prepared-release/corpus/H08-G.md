# H08-G | Cedar Vale — coal interpretation and data exceptions

SYNTHETIC DEMO MATERIAL — not a Capital Group record, policy, client or commitment.

Case: H08 | Date: 2026-05-08 | Owner: Historical FI PC and ESG | Version: 1.0 | Status: historical

## H08-G§1 — Coal interpretation in this case

Cedar Vale explicitly approved thermal-coal extraction revenue >=10%, with power generation out of scope for that mandate. Its client specified parent aggregation. This is a negotiated client rule, not an industry definition of no thermal coal.

## H08-G§2 — Unresolved issuer data

One issuer lacked ESG revenue classifications and another required parent mapping. Unknown values went to review; they were not treated as zero. Early review enabled the client to select cash substitution.

## H08-G§3 — Reuse limit

Offer the definition and test pattern as an option for Alpenridge review. Do not infer that no thermal coal permits up to 10% or excludes only extraction; the new client could intend any involvement, generation as well, or a named issuer list.
