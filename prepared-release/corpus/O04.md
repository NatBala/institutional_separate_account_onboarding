## O04§1 — Parallel preparation

Request indicative holdings and data-quality fields during intake. Obtain Legal/FI PC interpretation before a definitive exclusion screen. Custody and market-access work may proceed in parallel, but unresolved securities must have a client-approved disposition.

## O04§2 — Derivatives dependency

FX forwards and rate futures require relevant documentation, counterparty/broker and operational readiness. A staged bond-only plan is an option only if PM and client accept the investment implications and enforceable controls prevent premature activation.

## O04§3 — Schedule use

Case-study rework days measure workstream effort or elapsed remediation in the source record, not additive launch delay. Build an owner-reviewed dependency plan. Without current capacity, external response times and dated readiness evidence, no calibrated launch-date prediction is supported.
